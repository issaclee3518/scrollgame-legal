BBOX Account & Data Deletion (English)

How to delete your account in the BBOX app

  1. Open the BBOX app and sign in with Google or Apple.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and data.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately and cannot be restored.

If you cannot sign in, email us from your registered address and we will process the deletion within 7 days.

Data deleted: account and sign-in credentials, profile information, uploaded games and Storage files, likes, comments and saved items.

Data retained: transaction or billing records required by applicable law are kept for up to 5 years and then destroyed. No other data is retained.
